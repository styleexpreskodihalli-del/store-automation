Privacy Policy

Effective date: 18 August 2026

STore Automation, operated by Style Expres, respects the privacy of businesses and users using the platform.

1. Information We Collect

  • Name and account information
  • Email address and contact information
  • Business name, category, address, hours and business information
  • Business images and content supplied by customers
  • Information required for authorised third-party integrations
  • Technical, device and usage information required to operate and secure the platform
  • Payment and transaction identifiers received from payment providers

2. Google Business Profile

If a customer authorises Google Business Profile integration, STore may process the information and permissions required to provide the requested integration and automation services.

3. Payments

Payments are processed through authorised payment service providers such as Razorpay. STore does not intentionally store customers' card numbers, CVV numbers, UPI PINs or other payment-instrument credentials.

4. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide listings, operate accounts, deliver automation services, process payments, provide support, improve the platform, prevent fraud and maintain security.

5. Third-Party Services

Some STore functionality depends on third-party services. Their own terms and privacy policies may apply to information processed through those services.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse and loss.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and maintain legitimate business records.

8. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may request access, correction or deletion of personal information where applicable.

9. Policy Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published on this page.

10. Contact

Privacy-related requests may be submitted through the support/contact facility provided on the STore platform.
